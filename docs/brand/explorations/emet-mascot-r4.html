emet mascot options, round 4

Maintainer feedback on round 3: A and D stay, C is rejected, B, E and F are dropped, and A's chest socket read as a giant mouth. This round redraws A three ways (no mouth, a slit mouth, a small core in place of the mouth), keeps D and adds two refinements, and adds two hybrids of A's body with D's forehead mark. Same rules as before: one variable goes off to stop it and animates to work.

Palette 1 (moss, stone, sun-light) is the default; use the toggle for palette 2 (clay-grey, teal, leaf). The toggle recolours every figure, state, loop and banner.
