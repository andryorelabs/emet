emet logo, round 2

Five marks derived from the brand's own parts: the eye lamps, the sprout, the single notch, the on and off lamp, and the word emet losing a letter. The mascot stays a separate companion. Colours are placeholders: a neutral clay-grey and one moss lamp accent, so colour does not bias the choice. Wordmark in the T2 display face, Space Grotesk 700 at -0.045em.

Every mark is a 32 x 32 grid drawing in two fills, clay and accent. The one-colour panels set both to the same value; concepts 1 and 4 keep their lamps readable through a gap, the others merge.

All five at 16, 32 and 64 px

01

Twin lamps

The two eye lamps set into a clay slab with its single notch, the socket where the sprout grows. It is the mascot's face reduced to two holes and one bite, with no mouth, body or sprout drawn.

Small-size weakness. Closest to the mascot and to any robot-face emoji. At 16 px the lamp discs are about 2.6 px and the ring gap between lamp and hole is under 1 px, so it reads as a slab with two dots. The notch vanishes below 24 px.

02

Lamp sprout

The sprout whose bud is the lamp: growth and the lit eye in one stroke, standing on the moss base. The pairing is the mascot's only vertical element, so it is the brand's own, not a generic leaf.

Small-size weakness. Sprouts are common in eco and wellness marks. The leaf pair is about 4 px wide at 16 px and fills in. In one colour the lamp loses its accent role and the mark reads as a plant on a shelf.

03

Lit e

A lowercase e whose crossbar is the lamp: the word alive. Take the bar away and a c remains, the same erasure as emet to met, with no Hebrew letter drawn.

Small-size weakness. The most legible at 16 px, and the most generic: any e in a ring looks like a browser or a settings icon. The erased state only reads if the story is told. Weakest ownership of the five.

04

Forehead slot

The word written on the golem's forehead: a clay block with a slot, and one lit line inside it. One approved spec, one lit word, then it works.

Small-size weakness. Reads as a mailbox, a battery or an ID card before it reads as a golem. The lit line is 1.5 px at 16 px, so it nearly vanishes. Needs the story told.

05

Four, one lit

Four letters as four clay columns, the first one cut short and replaced by a lit lamp: emet losing a letter, and also four parallel workers with one awake.

Small-size weakness. Reads as a signal meter or a bar chart. The lamp dot is 2.9 px wide at 16 px, close to the column width, so the erased idea is easy to miss. Strong silhouette, weak story.
