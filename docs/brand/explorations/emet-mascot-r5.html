emet mascot options, round 5

Round 4 feedback: two items only. A1-M puts A2's slit mouth back on A1 and keeps the light in the eyes. COMBO keeps A1 as the lead, takes A2's slit mouth and H1's green base, and says what wins when they clash. The r4 originals A1, A2 and H1 are shown beside them. Same rule as before: one variable goes off to stop it and animates to work.

Palette 1 (moss, stone, sun-light) is the default; use the toggle for palette 2 (clay-grey, teal, leaf). The toggle recolours every figure, state, loop and banner.

Verdict

  • Golem: medium. Heavy bean, moss patch and a dour mouth; the sprout still leans it toward plant sprite.
  • Mouth: it does not read odd again. The slit is 4 units wide against the 7-unit ring that made the A mouth in round 3, and it sits well below the eyes, so it reads as a flat, deadpan line. One caveat: lit, haloed eyes over a flat mouth give a wide-eyed look that can read as startled in the working state; the dashed asleep state and the done state read calm.
  • Distinct: medium. It is A1 with a face line; the lamp eyes are the part that is not generic.
  • 16 px: the silhouette and the sprout hold; the mouth is under 1 px and vanishes, and the lamps are about 1 px each, so the light state mostly shows as a halo.
  • Childish: borderline. The mouth makes it look deadpan, which helps against cute, but round body plus sprout is still friendly.

Verdict

  • Clash and decision: A1 dominates, so its body, sprout and eye lamps stay untouched and the lamps remain the only variable. The slit mouth is static. H1's bean body and forehead glyph are not used, as asked; only its mossy hem is taken, as a plain green band at the foot. Nothing fights for the light, because only the eyes ever change.
  • Golem: medium. The green base grounds the bean and adds the overgrown mood; the sprout still leans it toward plant sprite.
  • Distinct: medium. It is A1-M plus a hem, so it differs from A1-M by one band of colour.
  • 16 px: reads cleanly. At 2x the silhouette, sprout, moss patch and green base are all legible and the three green features share one colour, so they do not compete. The slit mouth (under 1 px) disappears and the eye lamps are about 1 px, so the state shows as a halo, as in A1. In one colour the base vanishes and it collapses to A1-M.
  • Childish: borderline. The dour mouth helps; round body, sprout and foliage hem push back toward friendly.

Recommendation: COMBO, since the green base grounds the bean at no cost to the single eye-lamp variable; A1-M is the same figure without the base if the extra colour is unwanted.
