emet palette, round 2

Round 1 was rejected because it did not evoke clay or the golem. These four start from the approved mascot (COMBO) and its materials: clay-grey stone, moss and leaf green, and lamp light. Each has a cool neutral base and one accent that is not purple and not orange or terracotta. Type is T2: Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono, embedded in this file.

P1 Lamplamp light is the accent

The golem is dead clay until the lamp lights; the accent is that one word of light, a warm gold on cool stone.

P2 Mossmoss green is the accent

Clay with moss on it is the golem itself: a green-grey stone ground and the living green as the single voice of the brand.

P3 Teal-leafteal-leaf is the accent

A leaf-teal that sits between the moss and the lamp glow, over a near-neutral clay grey, so a real green stays free for ok.

P4 Stone-bluea stone-blue is the accent

River-stone slate with a washed blue, the cool side of fired clay: the most sober, with the golem's green kept as a quiet sage.
