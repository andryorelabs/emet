emet mascot options, round 3

New direction after rounds 1 and 2: a golem can sound old, but this is technology. Mood only: calm, large, gentle machines gone green, and solarpunk, where plants and devices share a roof, with warm light and clean energy. All forms are original. Every idea has one variable (a light, a plant or water) that goes off to stop it and animates to work. The mascot is a companion to the mark, not the logo.

Note: docs/brand/brief.md and naming.md were not present in any checkout, so the story is taken from the task text: a clay golem woken by one word, emet means truth, erase one letter and it stops.

Verdict

  • Golem: medium. A heavy bean of clay with a light in its chest reads as a made thing; the sprout pushes it toward plant sprite more than golem.
  • Distinct: medium. Bean body plus sprout is a common friendly-robot recipe; the moss patch and chest socket help.
  • 16 px: good. Body, sprout and socket survive; the dot is about 1 px, so awake and asleep differ by a single pixel of light.
  • Childish: borderline. Round, small, sprouting; it needs flat colour and a dry tone to stay sober.

P

Palette this direction suggests

Three oklch sets. No purple, no orange or terracotta accent.

1. Moss, stone, sun-light

stoneoklch(0.72 0.025 80)
mossoklch(0.58 0.10 135)
sun-lightoklch(0.88 0.15 95)
inkoklch(0.22 0.01 250)
paperoklch(0.96 0.006 100)

Best fit. Stone maps to white (7) and bright black (8), moss to green (2, 10), sun-light to bright yellow (11). Use bright yellow, not 3: many themes render plain yellow as brown or orange.

2. Clay-grey, teal, leaf

clay-greyoklch(0.70 0.03 70)
tealoklch(0.62 0.09 195)
leafoklch(0.76 0.17 135)
inkoklch(0.22 0.01 250)
paperoklch(0.96 0.006 100)

Good fit. Clay-grey to white (7), teal to cyan (6, 14), leaf to bright green (10). Two cool accents sit close in a terminal, so keep teal for structure and leaf for state.

3. Basalt, fern, glow-cyan

basaltoklch(0.40 0.015 250)
fernoklch(0.65 0.14 150)
glowoklch(0.90 0.12 190)
lichenoklch(0.80 0.09 120)
paperoklch(0.96 0.006 100)

Fits, but colder. Basalt to bright black (8), fern to green (2), glow to bright cyan (14). The glow loses its warmth, and the dark body is hard to see on a dark terminal.

On a 16-colour terminal oklch is approximated by the nearest ANSI slot, and each theme draws those slots differently. All three sets keep to green, yellow or cyan plus greys, so they survive; set 1 survives best.

Recommendation: D (forehead glyph) in palette 1, with B as the silhouette to revisit if a bigger, calmer body is wanted.
