Mascot directions

Four name-independent directions from docs/brand/mascot-research.md. Rough silhouettes only: options for the maintainer, not a decision. Each card shows the concept, the silhouette, a true 16 px render, a 32 px render and a one-colour preview on light and dark. Features are cut out as negative space, so every preview uses a single colour.

D1. Tiered stack

Three rounded slabs, narrow on top, with two dot eyes. Each slab is a wave: a dependency layer that lands before the next.

16 px
32 px
mono, dark

Pros

  • Most legible at 16 px; doubles as the simplified mark.
  • No animal collisions found among the ten cases.

Risks

  • Abstract: needs copy to explain it; may read as a cake or burger.
  • A boxy creature with eyes sits near the Claude Code block mascot: keep it rounded, no legs, no pixel grid, no orange.

D2. Worker ant

A colony of workers moving tickets in parallel under a coordinator. Large art shows the ant carrying a ticket card.

16 px
32 px
mono, dark

Pros

  • Clearest picture of the parallel-worker model.
  • Three body blobs still read at 16 px.

Risks

  • Insects read as "bugs" in software.
  • Legs and antennae vanish at 16 px.
  • Apache Ant owns "Ant" in build tools (logo not checked).

D3. Builder beaver

A beaver building a dam log by log: small careful pieces that add up to one structure, as tickets add up to one merged change.

16 px
32 px
mono, dark

Pros

  • Strong side silhouette: flat tail, round head.
  • "Careful builder" fits a tool that merges unattended.

Risks

  • Cute animals drift childish.
  • BuildStream, another build tool, has a beaver mascot.
  • Teeth and log detail are lost at 16 px.

D4. Herding dog

A border collie head with a white blaze: the coordinator that keeps many workers moving one way and gathers them at each wave.

16 px
32 px
mono, dark

Pros

  • Warm and trustworthy without being childish.
  • Symmetric front face; the blaze is one strong cut-out.

Risks

  • Dogs are common in branding (no trademark search run).
  • Carries less of the parallel-work idea than D1 or D2.
Research and sources: docs/brand/mascot-research.md and docs/brand/research-notes/mascots-cases.md. No network requests: inline SVG and system fonts only. Avoided on purpose: crabs, lobsters, claws, octopuses, block-pixel creatures, sparks and any orange or clay palette.
