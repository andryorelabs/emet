Voice

Control, sober and precise, with dry humour in small places. No hype.

We are

  • Controlled: one approval, then the work runs
  • Sober: plain verbs, short sentences
  • Precise: numbers, file names, exact commands
  • Dry: a small joke in the mascot or a terminal line
  • Honest: claims only what the CLI does today
  • Calm about agents: they run in isolation, nothing merges when flagged

We are not

  • Breathless: no "unleash", no "10x"
  • Grand: no "revolutionary", no "the future of coding"
  • Vague: no "seamless", no "magic"
  • Jokey: no puns in docs, errors or release notes
  • Promissory: no features that are not shipped
  • Anxious or boastful about autonomy

Tone by context

README, docs "One approval, then workers implement in isolated worktrees."
Terminal "Wave 2 held: one ticket flagged. Nothing merged."
Errors "CI failed on wave 1. PR left open for review."
Mascot "Lamp on. Still reading the spec."
Release notes "Adds forge init checks for missing config."
Social "Spec to merge, on one approval."

Avoid

Hype words

"AI-powered", "autonomous", "revolutionary", "supercharge", "ships while you sleep".

Claude visual clichés

Terracotta or orange, cream plus charcoal as the identity, a radial spark or starburst, a pixel creature that reads as Clawd.

Crowded territory

Octopus, ghost and hand mascots, and a purple accent.

Competitor naming

Vendor or model words in the name, factory, droid, squad, crew, orchestra, conductor, plain first names, and "Forge" as the lead word.

Unshipped claims

Anything the CLI does not do today.
