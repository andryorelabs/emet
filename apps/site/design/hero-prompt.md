# Hero picture prompt

Prompt used with ChatGPT image generation (the maintainer's account). Two references were attached: the mascot render (`docs/brand/mascot/mascot-work.svg`) and a rough layout sketch. The chosen result is `hero.webp`, version C.

Generate one wide 16:9 image: a hand-painted hero background for a premium software website.

I attached two images. The first is the brand character, "the emet golem": a small clay golem with a rounded tombstone-shaped body in pale warm grey clay, a cap of green moss on the top-left of the head and a skirt of moss around the base, a small sprout with two leaves on top, two small round glowing amber lamp eyes, and a thin horizontal slit mouth. The second is a rough layout sketch, to be used only as a composition guide, not as a style guide. In the final painting the golem must be much smaller than in the sketch.

Style: a hand-painted animation background, gouache and watercolour feel, soft diffused golden-hour light, atmospheric haze on distant ridges, gentle painterly brush texture. Rich but muted natural colours (moss green, sage, warm ochre, soft slate-blue sky). Calm, contemplative, high-end editorial quality. Inspired by the lush hand-painted landscapes of classic Japanese animation backgrounds, but it must not copy any specific film, scene or character.

Content: a wide, open valley seen from a slightly raised viewpoint. Terraced fields and groves of trees on the hillsides. Three small streams in the middle distance merge into one clear river flowing toward the viewer in the centre of the frame. Distant layered mountains in mist. Big soft cumulus clouds. The emet golem sits calmly on a grassy rise at the lower right third, in three-quarter view, looking out over the valley. It is painted in the same style and lit by the same light, clearly recognisable as the golem from the first image, and small in the frame: about 20 percent of the image height, not a close-up. Its eyes are soft and small, not cartoonish.

Composition: landscape orientation, 16:9. The top 45 percent of the image is a calm, low-detail sky (soft clouds and light only), because a headline will be placed over it. The centre column stays quiet. The strongest details are in the bottom third and the right third. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no watermark, no people, no other characters. At most two tiny distant birds.

Mood for this version: clear late-afternoon light: crisp air, long soft shadows across the fields, deep blue sky with big white cumulus clouds, more saturated but still natural greens.
