# Design

The visual identity of emet and where its source of truth lives.

- **Brand:** [docs/brand/](docs/brand/README.md) holds the brief, naming, voice, logo, mascot, terminal palette and the approved copy ([copy.md](docs/brand/copy.md)). Its colours and type are defined once in [docs/brand/tokens.css](docs/brand/tokens.css).
- **Website tokens:** [apps/site/src/styles/tokens.css](apps/site/src/styles/tokens.css) copies the brand tokens for the Website: the Moss palette as light-dark pairs and the type scale. Change the brand tokens first, then the Website copy.
- **Website design notes:** [apps/site/design/](apps/site/design/run-notes.md), and the canvases listed in [docs/design/README.md](docs/design/README.md).
- **Accessibility:** [ACCESSIBILITY.md](ACCESSIBILITY.md).
